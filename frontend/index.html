ATS Scoring Methodology

Our ATS Resume Analyzer uses a transparent, five-dimension scoring model grounded in natural language processing (spaCy) and semantic embeddings (Sentence Transformers).

1. Formatting & Structure (20 pts):

Validates standard resume sections (Experience, Education, Skills, Summary), bullet points, and linear document readability.

2. Keywords & Technical Skills (25 pts):

Accurately matches technical skills, programming languages, and tools using boundary-aware regex (handles C++, C#, .NET, Node.js).

3. Content Quality & Impact (25 pts):

Evaluates strong action verbs, quantifiable achievements ($ or % metrics), and rule-based text quality (typos, repeated words).

4. Skill Context Validation (15 pts):

Ensures claimed skills are corroborated with semantic context in your project descriptions and work history.

5. ATS Compatibility & Privacy (15 pts):

Checks document parseability while flagging privacy risks like full residential street addresses or postal codes.
